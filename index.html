Welcome to Shadyside

Welcome to the world of Fear Street Part One: 1994, a horror movie released in 2021. The story follows a group of teenagers who discover that the terrifying events in their town may be connected to a curse that has haunted Shadyside for centuries.

What Is Fear Street?

Shadyside is known for its violent history and mysterious murders. When a group of teenagers becomes the target of a killer, they begin investigating the dark secrets behind their town. Their discoveries reveal that the past may not be finished haunting the present.

What Makes This Movie Scary?
